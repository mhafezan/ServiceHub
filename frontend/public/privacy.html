ServiceHub privacy

ServiceHub uses Telegram identity, ride details and offers to arrange rides. Exact addresses are shared with your selected driver; the public channel displays only street and municipality. OpenAI processes private service conversations and relevant ride context to assist you. Google Places processes address searches.

Location sharing is optional for riders and required for driver trip controls. Share explicitly in Telegram or the Mini App. Participants can view each other's shared location only after departure and before the assignment ends. Stop Mini App sharing with its button; stop Telegram sharing in Telegram. Previously delivered location messages may remain in Telegram chat history.

Raw location samples are removed within 24 hours after the ride ends. Exact addresses and conversations are removed after 30 days. Minimal operational audit history is retained for 90 days. De-identified measured trip distance, accepted CAD price and your independently supplied municipality are retained for up to 365 days for future pricing research. Incomplete tracks are excluded from training exports. Google Maps content is not used for model training.

Backups expire separately under the operator's retention policy. Contact the channel administrator for support and deletion requests. Google features are subject to Google's privacy policy.
